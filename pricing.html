FAQ

Common questions

Is "from £797" the final price?

Every package is priced "from" its starting figure. Your exact price depends on what your business needs, and we confirm it on a free discovery call before any work begins.

Which package is right for my business?

Essential suits sole traders and new businesses needing to get online fast. Professional suits established restaurants, garages and salons wanting more visibility. Growth suits businesses actively losing work to competitors with better sites.

How long does a website take to build?

Essential sites launch in 7–10 days, Professional in 10–14 days, and Growth builds take 2–3 weeks.

What happens after my site goes live?

Every site launches onto a monthly care plan covering hosting, security and updates, starting at £97/month.

Can I add extra pages or features later?

Yes, extra pages are £150 each, and add-ons like a logo, Google Business Profile setup or an email welcome sequence can be added at any time.

How many rounds of revisions do I get?

Our Professional package includes 2 rounds of revisions. Essential and Growth builds are scoped individually on your discovery call.
