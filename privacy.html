Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026.

Who we are

This site is operated by Laara Digital LTD (company number 17408952), registered office 6 Darcy Close, Cheshunt, Broxbourne, Hertfordshire, EN8 8UQ. We're the data controller for the information described below. You can reach us at hello@laaradigital.co.uk or +44 7896 352996.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, through the quote form or short enquiry form on our Contact page. Depending on which form you use, that can include:

  • Your name, business name and location
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Details about your project: services you're interested in, whether you have an existing website, budget band, timeline and any notes you add
  • Your preferred way for us to get back in touch

We don't collect payment details, and we don't ask for anything beyond what's needed to understand your enquiry and reply to it.

Cookies and tracking

We don't run analytics, advertising pixels or any tracking cookies on this site. The only thing stored in your browser is the quote form's progress (which step you're on and what you've filled in so far), held in sessionStorage so you don't lose your answers if you accidentally reload the page. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and clears itself once you close the tab.

How we use it

We use what you submit to respond to your enquiry, put together a quote, and follow up about a project you've asked us about. We don't use it for marketing you haven't asked for, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Our legal basis for this is that processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), or, where that doesn't quite fit, our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries sent to us (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to us through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service that receives what you submit and emails it to us. We don't share your information with anyone else, and we don't use any other third-party processors on this site at present.

This site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Simply loading a page may generate standard server access logs (such as IP address and browser type) on GitHub's infrastructure as part of normal web hosting — we don't receive or process these logs ourselves.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and quote information for as long as it's useful for responding to you and, if we end up working together, for the life of that relationship. Where we don't hear back or a project doesn't go ahead, we aim to delete or anonymise what you sent us within 24 months of our last contact, unless we're required to keep something longer (for example, invoices we're obliged to retain for accounting purposes).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what information we hold about you, and give you a copy of it
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate or incomplete
  • Delete your information, or restrict how we use it
  • Provide your information in a portable format
  • Stop processing your information where we're relying on legitimate interest

To ask about any of these, email hello@laaradigital.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information are welcome at hello@laaradigital.co.uk, or through our Contact page.
