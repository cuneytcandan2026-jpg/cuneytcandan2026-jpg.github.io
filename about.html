11+ years in CRM and customer marketing, before founding Laara Digital.

Cuneyt spent that time helping businesses understand and grow their customer base. That means he doesn’t just design websites, he thinks about how they turn visitors into customers.

Laara Digital is built the way he wished agencies worked for the businesses he used to help: fast, honest, fixed pricing, and someone who explains every step in plain English, and sticks around afterwards.

The small studio advantage

What changes when there’s no layer between you and the work

  • Account manager relays your brief. → You talk directly to the person building it.
  • Weeks of sign-off layers. → One discovery call, then straight into the build.
  • One of hundreds of accounts. → A deliberately small number of projects, each one built and supported personally.
  • Support disappears after the invoice. → Care plans mean you’re never abandoned after launch.
  • Generic templates for any industry. → Sites built specifically for trades, salons, restaurants and gyms.
