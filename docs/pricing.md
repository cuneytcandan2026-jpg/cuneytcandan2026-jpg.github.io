## Strategy Notes

- Publish "websites from £797" — enough signal to qualify enquiries without locking in. Never quote without a discovery call first.
- Use the discovery call to justify Professional or Growth based on what the business is actually losing — missed calls, no bookings, embarrassment about the current site.
- Don't compete on price with £299 template mills. Win on speed (live in 10 days), personal service and understanding the customer — not just design.
- Goal: land most clients on Professional (£1,497) or above; Essential is the entry point, not the main offer.
- Review pricing after every 5 projects. As the portfolio grows, raise floors — not ceilings.
