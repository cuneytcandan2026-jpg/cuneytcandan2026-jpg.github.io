# LAARA Digital — Positioning Statement & USP

## The Positioning Statement

LAARA Digital helps small UK businesses get found online and grow.

We build fast, mobile-ready websites for trades, salons, restaurants, gyms and service businesses across the UK. Most of our clients have no website, an outdated site, or poor local visibility — they're losing enquiries to competitors. We fix that, then stay involved to keep the site fresh and competitive.

## The Problem We Solve

Small business owners are invisible online:

- No website, or a website that looks unprofessional
- Low visibility in local Google search
- Don't know who to trust or how to start
- Big agency quotes (£5k–£15k+) feel out of reach for what they need
- Get abandoned after launch with no support

## The Solution We Deliver

A complete, hands-off online presence:

- Professional website, live in as little as 7–10 days
- Local SEO so they show up for local searches
- Mobile-ready (most of their customers visit on their phone)
- Clear, fixed pricing — no surprises, no upselling
- Ongoing care plans so the site stays competitive and updated

## Why Choose LAARA Digital

- **We specialise in your industry** — trades, salons, restaurants, gyms, cleaning companies. We know what works for you, not generic web design.
- **Fast delivery** — live in 7–10 days for a straightforward build, up to 3 weeks for a full Growth site. A quick discovery call, then straight into the build.
- **No corporate jargon** — we speak plainly. You understand every step.
- **We stay involved** — care plans mean you're never abandoned. We keep your site fresh, secure and optimised.
- **Clear pricing** — fixed packages (£797, £1,497, £2,497), fully scoped up front. No hidden costs.
- **UK-wide** — we work with local small businesses across the UK and understand what makes them convert.

## The USP (In One Sentence)

"Websites built specifically for local UK trades and service businesses — live in days not months, backed by industry know-how and ongoing support most agencies don't offer."

## How We Describe It in Sales Conversations

"We build professional websites for small businesses like yours — trades, salons, restaurants, gyms — that get you found on Google and bring in more enquiries. We move fast, keep things simple, and stick around to keep your site competitive."
